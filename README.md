## 💡 Best practices for better results

The quality of your output depends on the instructions you provide. Follow these tips to get the best text:

*   **Give context:** Tell the app what you sell. Include the biggest benefit of your product.
*   **Be clear:** Use simple language in your prompt. Avoid complex jargon unless necessary.
*   **Specify goals:** Tell the app what you want the reader to do. Should they click a link, call a number, or sign up for a list?
*   **Refine:** If the first draft is not perfect, ask the app to make it shorter or more conversational. You can edit the prompt and generate new versions as many times as you like.
